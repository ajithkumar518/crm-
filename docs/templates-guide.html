Sections explained

  1. Header — Company logo, full address, document number, date, contact person, contact number, payment & delivery terms, and delivery period.
  2. Bill To / Ship To — Customer name, customer code, billing/shipping address, city/state, and GSTIN.

Sections explained

  1. Header — Company logo and address, proforma number, date, validity date, quotation reference, contact person, contact number, and payment/delivery terms.
  2. Bill To / Ship To — Customer and shipping party details including code, address, state, and GSTIN. Separate bill-to and ship-to parties are supported.
  3. Items Table — Additional columns include Item Code, Length in mm, and Delivery Days. The last row shows the item total before extra charges.
  4. Tax Treatment — Intra-state uses CGST + SGST. Inter-state automatically switches to a single IGST column. Missing ship-to state blocks proforma generation.
  5. Bottom Left — Comments, amount in words, and terms & conditions.
  6. Summary Box — Taxable value, total tax, extra charges (cutting, other, weighing, delivery, testing), and grand total.
  7. Signature — Authorized signatory block.
